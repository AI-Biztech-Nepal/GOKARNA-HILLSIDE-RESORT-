Welcome

A quiet hillside sanctuary

Where the city fades
and the hills begin

Tucked into the terraced slopes of Jagadol in Gokarna, our resort sits just far enough from Kathmandu's bustle to feel like another world — pine-scented air, birdsong at dawn, and views that stretch across forested ridgelines. This is a place built for slowing down.

  • Hillside setting — private grounds surrounded by pine and rhododendron forest
  • Comfortable accommodation — rooms designed for genuine rest
  • Warm hospitality — genuine Nepalese care, in every detail
